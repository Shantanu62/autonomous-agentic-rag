
COMPANY OPERATIONS STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE

Document: Employee Leave Policy
Department: Human Resources
Version: 1.0

1. Annual Leave
Every full-time employee receives 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.

3. Leave Approval
Employees must submit annual leave requests at least 5 working days before the requested leave date.

4. Emergency Leave
Emergency leave requests must be communicated to the reporting manager as soon as possible.

5. Approval Authority
The reporting manager is responsible for reviewing and approving leave requests.

6. Public Holidays
Official company holidays are separate from annual leave balances.
